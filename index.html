    Rules

    Packing

    Tap a belt crate to select it. Tap it again to rotate. Only a real drag onto a valid cell seats it — taps never place. Hold Scrap to dump a blocker. Hold a selected crate to scrap it too.

    Cars

    Boxcar takes anything. Flatbed is wide and short — timber likes it, but pile the mass on one side and she tips.

    Reefer is food only (Peach, Ice). Tanker is hazmat only (Drum, Barrel).

    Consist

    Car order is a coupling. Tanker against Reefer, or a food car hugging a hazmat load, dings safety. Reorder on the waybill before the clock starts.

    Safety

    Never seat hazmat next to food. One bad pairing drops the safety meter and the payout. Crew morale is the third meter — scrap spam, a crowded belt, and Loader abuse sour the next belt.

    Schedule

    Each train is a ticket: 2–3 contracts on the Depart strip. 1★ is an on-time loaded depart. 3★ is every contract. Empty trains do not open the map. Night shift wants a 3★. Prairie stays calm. Later yards turn the screws — mid-run a car can be held for inspection; seat the freight they asked for on that grid. Last stretch the clock warns amber, then goes frantic under 12s.

    Depart early underfull for Fast Freight, or hold for density and risk the whistle.

    Loader

    Loader burst speeds the belt and helps pieces snap into a legal fit. It cools down after.

    Night shift

    After Harbor, night is optional: meaner clock, better pay. Toggle it on the map.
